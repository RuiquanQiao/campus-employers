Germany, healthcare: employers that belong and are not yet written (list-only pass, 2026-11)

- Landesärztekammern, Bundesärztekammer, Kassenzahnärztliche Vereinigungen
- District and municipal hospitals not yet listed (several hundred Kreiskliniken; the largest are written)

- Elderly care operators: Emeis Deutschland (formerly Orpea), Hansa Gruppe, Alpenland, Azurit, Benevit, Schönes Leben, Home Instead (franchise)
- Dental, eye and radiology chains (Colosseum Dental, Sanoptis, Artemis, radiology MVZ groups)
